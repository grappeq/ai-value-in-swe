# Re-derivation R1: what the refreshed dataset changes

**2026-07-06 · after refresh cycle R1 (three gap sweeps, 13 blind-extracted sources, 21 new evidence rows; table at 202 rows / 50 sources) · method pre-registered in LEDGER.md before the counter-evidence sweep reported**

Discipline: each verdict re-examined against the full refreshed table asking "what would we conclude from scratch?" — not "does the new evidence fit?". Movements listed first; confirmations second. Blind extraction meant the new records were graded without knowledge of prior conclusions; several came back *different* from what triage predicted, in both directions, which is evidence the guard worked.

## Movements (applied to the memos and outputs)

**M1 — Tension 4, duplication leg: from "weakest-supported" to "contradicted by measured evidence."** The refreshed table has four designs against the popular duplication narrative (agents-vs-ides null, cursor-quality null, agent-maintainability null-as-predictor, and now mao-2026's reversal — AI-attributed code *less* duplicated, 14.29% vs 27.57%) versus one COI'd population trend (GitClear) for it. From scratch, no honest reader would call this leg "contested": the measured evidence contradicts it. Overall tension-4 verdict unchanged (contested-but-substantiated, medium) — the two Tier A legs (complexity growth; downstream maintenance cost) are untouched, and readability-agents-2026 mildly reinforces the complexity leg (AIDev cluster, one voice).

**M2 — Tension 2 gains a work-type split: "brownfield" is not one thing.** Google migrations (74–80% AI-authored, measured; time savings self-reported) and Nubank (~12x, estimate-basis, one data point) both show agent pipelines performing well on *mechanical, well-specified transformations inside old codebases* (T5: migrations, dependency upgrades). Neither is strong evidence (C/D, self-reported counterfactuals, two voices of which one is vendor-anchored) — but from scratch, the failure gradient reads as "open-ended contextual work in mature codebases" (T4), not "old code" per se. The attenuation direction stands unanimous; the operative variable looks like task contextuality × codebase maturity, not maturity alone. Confidence on the refinement: low-medium (Tier C/D only).

**M3 — Tension 7 and RQ2: the citation-corruption finding upgrades from "pattern" to "systemic", with two new failure classes.** R1 added: a fully **phantom consultancy report** (bain-2026 — laundered via SEO aggregators, figures misattributed even within the laundering chain); a vendor inflating **its own measured result** ~1.5x in its own marketing (atlassian: 45% headline vs 30.8% in its own ICSE paper); vendor-only numbers circulating as customer-corroborated (nubank: only "12x" survives corroboration); and secondary reporting quoting a paper's adoption-month *peak* as its steady state (quispe: "+41 commits" ≈ 2x the average effect). Count now ~14 instances across every layer of the evidence ecosystem — press, vendors, aggregators, and consultancy attribution. New anti-metric for the RQ6 list: **"time to first review comment"** (definitionally zero once an AI auto-comments on every PR — Atlassian's own case).

**M4 — RQ1's absence claim narrows: five unmeasured task types become three.** T11 now has direct evidence (ai-build-code-2026: 364 smells but 824/945 files smell-free, 61.4% merged with minimal intervention — B weak-end, AIDev cluster) and T10 has thin evidence (comment-quality study, plus readability's documentation touch). T1, T2, T9 remain confirmed empty after targeted hunting — which is now a stronger claim than before, because we looked and documented dry holes.

**M5 — Tension 1's internal-incoherence sub-claim reaches three voices.** Sergeyuk (82.3% perceive gains while ~50% perceive no change on the axes where telemetry diverges most — "silent restructuring"), beyond-the-commit (86% satisfied vs ~60% reporting <1hr/week saved), and vella-blincoe (productivity–experience decoupling) now jointly show self-report is internally inconsistent even before any ground-truth comparison. The four paired designs and the split verdict stand unchanged.

## Confirmations (no change warranted)

- **Tension 6 (bottleneck displacement):** the challengers corroborated it instead. Plandek's own material says "bottlenecks shift, but overall delivery outcomes barely change"; Mao adds an independent measured absorption signal (AI-commit handoff latency 28.28 vs 11.23 days); Atlassian shows automation compressing review *wait* while human review volume falls and AI comments resolve less often — queue absorbed, function still unproven. Verdict stands: confirmed in direction, magnitude unresolved.
- **Tension 3 (throughput/stability):** Atlassian's measured −30.8% cycle time is a genuine counter-direction datapoint for one construct (review cycle, single-purpose agent, vendor-measured); Mao's stabilisation-time finding cuts the other way. Still contested; the constructs still don't line up.
- **Tension 5 (seniority):** German survey adds only a perception split (juniors rate prompting effective 78% vs seniors 39%). Insufficient evidence stands; the two measured E3 datapoints still disagree on shape.
- **RQ4 (cost):** nothing new survived — the one promising lead (Bain) was a phantom, which *reinforces* the scarcity verdict. Closed as before.
- **RQ5 (translation):** texture added (M2's work-type split; Atlassian and Plandek as direction-only vendor datapoints), no reversal. The verdict paragraph and medium confidence stand.
- **The scarcity headline:** Quispe is the only new measured E3 quasi-experiment, is self-labelled associational, uses activity metrics only, and decays fast — the "no rigorous agentic RCT beyond METR" claim survives its strongest test yet. Notably, Quispe's spike-then-decay pattern converges with the CMU transient-velocity story from a fully independent voice (Caltech, own pipeline, not AIDev) — the first genuinely independent corroboration of transience.
- **Three conclusions attacked by the adversarial sweep survived with nothing found:** review bottleneck, self-report unreliability, no-missed-RCT.

## Verdict on the refresh

No direction-level conclusion moved. Five substantive refinements did — exactly the bound-and-caveat-level movement predicted before the refresh began (and priced into the stated confidence levels). The refresh's largest single contribution is epistemic: the counter-evidence sweep was run, its strongest challengers were read blind, and the conclusions now carry "attacked and survived" status rather than "never attacked".
