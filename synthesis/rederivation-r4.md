## B. Movements on the open questions (statuses applied in outputs/follow-up-questions.md)

**Q1 (−19% vs 1.5–1.7×) — PARTIALLY ANSWERED, unchanged.** No significant causal E3 estimate appeared. Demirer's sync-agent PR effect (+86%, OSS, time-shifted matched controls) sits inside the he-2026 corridor at per-adopter level and is consistent with the dilution story at organisation level (dx-reock +7.8%) — but it is activity, not task time, and its magnitudes are unstable across revisions. Reconciliation unchanged: tool-generation × setting × horizon × selection × *unit of output*.

**Q2 (heavy use vs underperformance) — OPEN, one new angle.** dont-vibe-control-2026 (C, E3) documents *control patterns* rather than volume: 9/13 professionals review every change in their own domain, chunk plans into ≤6 steps, and drop code review on unfamiliar tasks (3/13 monitor output instead). That is the first qualitative description of the usage-pattern variable Q2 asks for; no outcome attached. Demirer's activity-quartile heterogeneity (low-activity developers gain most in relative terms) is volume, not pattern.

**Q3 (does quality debt cash out?) — OPEN; narrowed again on two of three channels.**
(a) *Catastrophic tail*: the 0.07% figure (dev-agent-misalignment-2026) is now known to rest on n=11 episodes on the paper's weakest-validated axis (judge accuracy 0.64 [0.54, 0.73]) — low precision; state it as "of the order of 0.1%, imprecise", never as a measured rate.
(b) *Structural debt*: two new persistence rows, both baseline-free or one-voice — 22.7% of AI-introduced static issues survive at HEAD (debt-behind-ai-boom-2026, no human comparator) and merged agent PRs draw verified fixes at 1.62× the odds of human PRs (who-finishes-the-job-2026, AIDev-pop, unmatched) with a low absolute incidence (4.5% in 30 days). The maintenance question now carries an *intra-dataset contradiction* (NAIST "less maintenance received" vs Deakin "more fixes received"; 83% human maintenance vs 69.6% same-agent fix PRs) — different units, one provenance, logged in contradictions.md. Committed AI configuration halves the complexity cost in one voice (RAMP, Stanford–CMU; hypothesis-generating, 3.8% pre-adoption configuration — discipline marker not excluded).
(c) *Detectability*: **now two independent provenances.** With the model held constant, harness releases produced +52%/+131% token spikes and a −6.9pp resolve-rate drop that passed all >300 of the project's CI checks (harness-evolution-2026, A-for-rigour, lab). Together with Anthropic's two missed regressions this is no longer a vendor anecdote: standard test suites do not see agent-quality drift. Mechanism confidence medium.
Still true from scratch: no organisation publishes defect/incident rates pre/post agentic adoption (Duolingo's "for the most part, no" is informal revert observation; Qodo's 89% is recalled prevalence among screened adopters). The pilot (Q10) remains the only route to a denominator.

**Q4 (skill formation) — watch PARTIALLY MET.** Not a replication of Shen & Tamkin, but an independent agent-condition conceptual replication: 54 students, randomised, agent arm faster and more accurate initially, comprehension lower (d>0.8, p<0.002), extension-task null at 20 minutes, preference ignores understanding (impaired-programming-2026; UMD/NYU/CMU, NSF). The theme now has two randomised legs from independent groups and its first agent-modality one — both students, both single-session. Still no longitudinal professional study.

**Q5 (unmeasured task types) — T2 moves; T8 gains a second non-E3 source.** T2 (architecture/design) moves from "confirmed dry" to "E2 usage/perception evidence only, zero outcome evidence" (291 ChatGPT design conversations + 65 practitioners; architecture tasks median 11 rounds). T8 gains Google's Auto-Diagnose (E2 single-pass, Google-on-Google, C): expert accuracy 90% vs user helpfulness 63%, no time-saved measurement — T8 still has zero measured E3 effect. T1 and T9 remain confirmed dry (the one T1 lead, a WER 2026 chat-log paper, was not machine-readable this cycle).

**Q6 (seniority) — CLOSED, unchanged.** The only new seniority-adjacent evidence is E1 (hoffmann-2024: lower-prominence developers shift effort more, Microsoft–GitHub cluster) and E1/E2 corpus counts (wittig: seniors mixed across five papers in five directions). Nothing at E3.

**Q7 (what made the 2×-mandate firm work?) — PARTIALLY ANSWERED, one candidate condition added, one case added.** Sixth candidate: committed AI configuration (RAMP) — one voice, observational, hypothesis-generating. Itaú's one-person squad (C, participant-authored) reads as a catalogue of the earlier conditions (narrow scope, strong specification, deep institutional knowledge, mandatory review, verification harness) and adds a seventh observed displacement (lead time per story unchanged). A second enterprise DiD still does not exist.

**Q8 (review bottleneck: queue vs function) — REINFORCED; now has an independent academic leg.** zhong-2026 (own-mined, 207 projects, no AIDev data): AI-reviewer involvement cuts time-to-decision (−2.5 to −4.5 days/KLOC) with a flat-to-worse process-smell proxy. LinearB (D) adds that agentic PRs wait 5.25× longer for first review and are reviewed 1.5× *slower* once picked up. Duolingo's auto-approval bot and Itaú's homologation bottleneck are two more routing-around/absorption cases. Hoffmann (E1) supplies the oldest version of the mechanism: effort shifts away from coordination/review events toward code pushes. No restored-review case exists.

**Q9 (J-curve) — OPEN; hole persists.** CircleCI's Q2 Pulse shows a vendor telemetry series moving *back toward baseline* (main-branch success 70.8% → 76.7%; median main-branch throughput flat after −6.8%) — but with no AI variable and unconfirmed like-for-like windows it is not a J-curve observation, only the absence of a durable CI-level decline. Nothing in 25 sources shows dip-then-recovery in any organisation's own data.

**Q10 (run your own pilot) — unchanged, strengthened a third time** by the detectability finding's second provenance: the complaint/anomaly channel is now backed by a lab result, not only a vendor postmortem.

**Q11 (spend caps → output) — OPEN, confirmed unmeasured** (Route 1: nothing for Uber or Microsoft after June 2026).

**Q12 (consumption curve) — OPEN; a tool-layer mechanism added.** Tokens per task rose ~70% across 35 harness releases at a flat resolve rate (harness-evolution-2026) — consumption can grow with no change in model, price, usage volume or output. Uber's "AI cost up 6× since 2024" (method-free) and Gartner's general September update add no coding-specific curve.

**Q13 (independence crisis) — DISCHARGED; evidence base enlarged.** Corruption-in-transit instances 17 → 25 (eight new, listed in §E); a new sub-pattern (point-estimate instability across preprint revisions: Quispe ×3, Demirer 40–80%); a cluster merger (Stanford and CMU co-author RAMP on the reused agents-vs-ides panel — one provenance from now on); the AIDev monoculture now contradicts itself; and a separate internal-inconsistency class (four papers/reports whose own numbers disagree) recorded but not counted.

**New Q14 — Is the writing-vs-shipping attenuation an open-source artefact?** Demirer's chain (commits +153% → releases +20%) is the first large-N measurement of the gap that tension 6 predicted, but "release" is a GitHub Releases object on public repositories. Whether the same attenuation holds inside an enterprise delivery pipeline — where he-2026 saw AI-written changes ship 22% *slower* and Itaú saw lead time unchanged — is the open empirical question that would settle tension 6's magnitude. *Chase: any enterprise reporting commits/PRs and deployment frequency on the same adopter cohort; otherwise the pilot's delivery-vs-activity pair.*

## C. Tension-verdict movements (addenda applied to memos + index)

- **T1 (perception gap): operative claim RESOLVED stands; two new datapoint kinds appended.** (i) Preference-vs-understanding: students rated the agent far more helpful (4.7 vs 3.3) and lower-effort while measuring lower on comprehension — and their self-rated understanding was *accurate*; the gap is between what they chose and what they knew (impaired-programming-2026). (ii) Expert-vs-user: 90% expert-judged diagnosis accuracy vs 63% user helpfulness on the same outputs — perception *below* measurement (google-autodiagnose-2026). Corpus-level: perceived findings 71% positive vs objective 55% (wittig-2026; overlaps our primaries — aggregation, not a new voice). Direction-generality verdict (low) stands: the gap runs both ways.
- **T2 (greenfield vs brownfield): CONTESTED on magnitude, direction unanimous — unchanged; perception map added.** Professionals' own suitability counts (boilerplate 25:0, tests 19:2, docs 20:0 suitable; legacy integration 3:17, business logic 2:15 unsuitable; architecture contested 13:23 — dont-vibe-control-2026) mirror the measured map exactly. One positive E3 brownfield case (Itaú; participant-authored; sprint 1 was greenfield scaffolding) joins the T5-mechanical-migration "favourable brownfield" refinement without changing it.
- **T3 (throughput vs stability): CONTESTED — unchanged; one series added, Amazon still unresolved.** CircleCI's two 2026 reports (D, no AI variable) show main-branch success at a five-year low in Sept 2025 (70.8%) recovering by March 2026 (76.7%) with median main-branch throughput flat — a vendor-interpreted pattern, consistent with "no durable stability decline" at CI level. Qodo's 89% recalled-incident prevalence is hype-baseline colour. Amazon: no new primary.
- **T4 (quality debt): CONTESTED-BUT-SUBSTANTIATED (medium) stands; leg map updated.** Persistence leg gains two directional rows (22.7% static-issue survival, baseline-free; 1.62× fix odds, one-voice, low absolute incidence); maintenance leg now carries an intra-dataset contradiction (volume received vs fixes received); the configuration-moderation hint (RAMP) is one voice and observational; the **detectability leg gains an independent lab provenance** (harness regressions passing full CI) — this is the leg that moved. Churn: Demirer's "deleted lines grow faster than added lines" is a different construct and is noted, not promoted. Duplication leg: unchanged (contradicted).
- **T5 (seniority): INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE — chase closed, unchanged.** Hoffmann (E1, Microsoft–GitHub cluster) strengthens the pre-agentic floor-lift *direction* within one cluster; Demirer's activity-quartile gradient is not seniority. No E3 third voice.
- **T6 (bottleneck displacement): CONFIRMED in direction — confidence on direction raised to HIGH; magnitude moves from "unresolved" to "bounded at open-source scale, one cluster".** From scratch: seven named organisations (Agoda, Zendesk, the 2× firm, Cloudflare, Salesforce, Itaú, Duolingo), three vendor telemetry sets (Faros, DX, LinearB), two CI-vendor reports (CircleCI, no AI variable), one platform (GitHub volumes) and now a 500,000-developer matched event study (Demirer: sync-agent commits +153% → PRs +86% → repos +47% → releases +20%; autocomplete +30% → +9% n.s.) all point one way, and Hoffmann supplies the E1 ancestor of the mechanism (effort shifts from coordination to code pushes). No source anywhere shows the opposite. Magnitude caveats travel: Demirer is Microsoft telemetry with Microsoft-consultant authors (one cluster with Peng/Cui/Heilman/Quispe), counts GitHub Releases objects, carries no quality outcome, and its magnitudes moved 40–80% between revisions; he-2026 remains the only enterprise causal estimate. Q14 is the open magnitude question.
- **T7 (bad metrics): RESOLVED stands; three exhibits added.** "% AI-generated code": a 15,000-developer survey's self-reported shares sum to 112% (jetbrains-2026). Review duration: merge-time review signals did not predict later fixes (who-finishes-the-job-2026). Vendor "elite/good" benchmark tiers are customer percentile ranks, not outcome-validated (linearb-2026). Three distinct "11%" figures circulate for Uber under one label (uber-autocover-2026).
